## What happened
The second edition of Digital Summit Angola is scheduled for 24 and 25 November 2026 at the InterContinental hotel in Luanda. According to the organisers, the event will look at four sectors: Energy and Strategic Resources; Digital Infrastructure and Connectivity; Finance and Insurance; and Mobility and Logistics. Artificial intelligence has also been identified as one of the priorities for discussion.

The organisers' stated goal is to bring the technology sector closer to the concrete needs of the economy's productive sectors.

## Why it matters
Digital transformation delivers more when it starts from each sector's real problems: managing energy networks, making payments faster and safer, improving logistics efficiency or making data available for decisions. Events that bring technology providers, companies and regulators together help identify those priorities and build partnerships.

The four chosen areas are ones where technology has a direct impact on costs, productivity and the quality of services delivered to citizens.

## What it means for companies and institutions in Angola
For companies in these sectors, this is a good moment to review processes and ask where digitalisation brings immediate gains: systems integration, automation of repetitive tasks, performance dashboards or electronic payments. Starting from a concrete, measurable problem usually works better than large projects without clear goals.

For public institutions, coordination across sectors — shared data, interoperable systems and clear rules — is what allows technology investment to translate into simpler, faster services.
