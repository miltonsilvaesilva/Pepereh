## What happened
Since 16 November 2026, Luanda has hosted the Angolan edition of Global Entrepreneurship Week, organised by Acelera Angola. This year's edition coincides with the incubator's tenth anniversary — it began operating in 2016 — under the motto "Leading Tomorrow, Today".

According to the organisers, as reported by the Angolan press, Acelera Angola has trained more than 10,000 entrepreneurs over the decade. Global Entrepreneurship Week is an initiative of the Global Entrepreneurship Network, launched in 2008 and held every November in many countries.

## Why it matters
Entrepreneurship is one of the most direct ways to create jobs, diversify the economy and turn ideas into products and services. Incubation and acceleration programmes help address one of new businesses' main weaknesses: the lack of structure, method and support networks in their early years.

At the same time, the Angolan business press has stressed that access to finance remains an unresolved challenge for many small businesses and startups.

## What it means for companies and institutions in Angola
For entrepreneurs, initiatives like this are a chance to validate ideas, learn from other founders and reach potential partners and investors. Preparation makes the difference: a clear business model, organised numbers and a value proposition that is easy to explain.

For established companies, the startup ecosystem is a source of innovation that can be tapped through pilot projects, partnerships and supply of solutions. For public and private institutions, continued support — training, mentoring and financing — is what turns good ideas into sustainable businesses.
